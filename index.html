Welcome!

This is the Madison Gamer Club and we’re looking to recruit new members! The MGC is a social club for adult gamers within the Madison area to meet and socialize with other adult gamers!

We meet up at least twice a month, on the 1st Saturday of the month and the 3rd Sunday of the month. Saturday meetups tend to be a specific activity usually at a new location, while Sunday meetups are always at Aftershock Arcade and are more vibes-based with a general theme.

More information can be found in our FAQ.

The MGC is primarily based on our Discord server. There is an application process, but so long as you put in effort in your application, you’ll definitely be accepted.
